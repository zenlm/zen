19:T1a4b,<p>Three years ago, we started Zen with a simple belief: AI should be open, decentralized, and governed by its community. Today we formalize that mission with the launch of Zoo Labs Foundation.</p>
<h2 id="why-a-foundation">Why a Foundation?</h2>
<p>The work we're doing transcends any single company. Open AI development, decentralized science, and community governance require sustained, mission-driven effort. A foundation structure ensures:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mission permanence</strong>: The foundation's charter protects the mission from commercial pressures</li>
<li><strong>Community ownership</strong>: Governance tokens give stakeholders genuine control</li>
<li><strong>Research independence</strong>: Researchers pursue important problems, not just profitable ones</li>
<li><strong>Open infrastructure</strong>: Tools and systems remain open and accessible</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-foundations-mission">The Foundation's Mission</h2>
<p>Zoo Labs Foundation exists to advance open AI research and decentralized science. We pursue this through:</p>
<h3 id="open-research">Open Research</h3>
<p>All research conducted under the foundation is published openly. This includes:</p>
<ul>
<li>Model weights and training code</li>
<li>Dataset documentation and curation tools</li>
<li>Benchmark results and evaluation frameworks</li>
<li>Negative results and failed experiments</li>
</ul>
<p>Transparency accelerates progress. Hiding failures wastes collective effort.</p>
<h3 id="decentralized-infrastructure">Decentralized Infrastructure</h3>
<p>We build and maintain infrastructure for decentralized AI:</p>
<ul>
<li>Federated training networks</li>
<li>Verifiable inference systems</li>
<li>Agent identity frameworks</li>
<li>Distributed compute protocols</li>
</ul>
<p>This infrastructure enables others to build decentralized AI applications without starting from scratch.</p>
<h3 id="community-governance">Community Governance</h3>
<p>Decisions about foundation direction are made by the community:</p>
<ul>
<li>ZIPs (Zoo Improvement Proposals) for major changes</li>
<li>Token-weighted voting for resource allocation</li>
<li>Working groups for focused initiatives</li>
<li>Open forums for discussion and debate</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="structure">Structure</h2>
<p>The foundation operates through several bodies:</p>
<h3 id="research-labs">Research Labs</h3>
<p>Full-time researchers pursuing core AI and DeSci research. Current focus areas:</p>
<ul>
<li>Large language models</li>
<li>Multimodal understanding</li>
<li>Alignment and safety</li>
<li>Cryptographic verification</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="grants-program">Grants Program</h3>
<p>Funding for external researchers and projects aligned with the mission. Initial fund: $10M over three years.</p>
<p>Priority areas:</p>
<ul>
<li>Open model development</li>
<li>Decentralized training methods</li>
<li>AI safety research</li>
<li>DeSci infrastructure</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="working-groups">Working Groups</h3>
<p>Community-led groups focusing on specific areas:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Model Development</strong>: Coordinating open model training</li>
<li><strong>Infrastructure</strong>: Building and maintaining shared tools</li>
<li><strong>Governance</strong>: Improving foundation governance</li>
<li><strong>Outreach</strong>: Education and community growth</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="initial-projects">Initial Projects</h2>
<p>The foundation launches with several active projects:</p>
<h3 id="zen-20">Zen 2.0</h3>
<p>Our next-generation language model series. Currently training:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Zen-2-7B</strong>: General-purpose base model</li>
<li><strong>Zen-2-34B</strong>: Enhanced reasoning and knowledge</li>
<li><strong>Zen-2-70B</strong>: Frontier capabilities (in planning)</li>
</ul>
<p>All models released under Apache 2.0.</p>
<h3 id="training-gym">Training Gym</h3>
<p>Open source infrastructure for model training. Now includes:</p>
<ul>
<li>Distributed training framework</li>
<li>Evaluation harness</li>
<li>Experiment tracking</li>
<li>Model registry</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="experience-ledgers">Experience Ledgers</h3>
<p>Persistent memory for AI agents. Production release scheduled for Q2 2024.</p>
<h3 id="proof-of-ai">Proof of AI</h3>
<p>Verifiable inference framework. SDK available now for models up to 500M parameters.</p>
<h2 id="governance">Governance</h2>
<h3 id="zen-token">ZEN Token</h3>
<p>The ZEN token governs the foundation:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Voting</strong>: Token holders vote on proposals</li>
<li><strong>Delegation</strong>: Delegate votes to representatives</li>
<li><strong>Staking</strong>: Stake tokens to participate in working groups</li>
</ul>
<p>Token distribution:</p>
<ul>
<li>40% Community treasury (vested over 4 years)</li>
<li>25% Early contributors</li>
<li>20% Foundation reserve</li>
<li>15% Grants program</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="zips-process">ZIPs Process</h3>
<p>Major decisions go through the ZIPs process:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Draft</strong>: Author writes proposal</li>
<li><strong>Discussion</strong>: Community feedback (minimum 2 weeks)</li>
<li><strong>Revision</strong>: Author incorporates feedback</li>
<li><strong>Vote</strong>: Token holders vote (5-day voting period)</li>
<li><strong>Implementation</strong>: Approved proposals are executed</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="transparency">Transparency</h3>
<p>All foundation operations are transparent:</p>
<ul>
<li>Financial reports published quarterly</li>
<li>Research progress shared monthly</li>
<li>Governance votes recorded on chain</li>
<li>Meeting notes published publicly</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="join-us">Join Us</h2>
<p>The foundation is as strong as its community. Ways to participate:</p>
<h3 id="contribute">Contribute</h3>
<ul>
<li>Submit research proposals</li>
<li>Contribute code to open projects</li>
<li>Participate in governance</li>
<li>Join working groups</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="build">Build</h3>
<ul>
<li>Use foundation infrastructure</li>
<li>Build on open models</li>
<li>Integrate with our tools</li>
<li>Launch complementary projects</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="support">Support</h3>
<ul>
<li>Acquire ZEN tokens</li>
<li>Provide compute resources</li>
<li>Fund specific research</li>
<li>Spread the word</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-road-ahead">The Road Ahead</h2>
<p>We're at the beginning. The foundation launches with a strong base: years of research, working infrastructure, and a committed community. But the hardest work is ahead.</p>
<p>Building open, decentralized AI systems that benefit everyone is ambitious. It requires sustained effort, careful governance, and broad participation. The foundation provides the structure. The community provides the energy.</p>
<p>Join us at zoo.ngo.</p>
