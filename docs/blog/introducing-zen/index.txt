19:T8d6,<p>Today we're announcing Zen, an open AI research initiative from Zoo Labs Foundation.</p>
<h2 id="why-zen">Why Zen?</h2>
<p>The current trajectory of AI development concentrates power in a handful of organizations. Models are trained on humanity's collective knowledge, yet the resulting systems remain proprietary. We believe there's a better path.</p>
<p>Zen represents our commitment to building AI that is:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Open</strong>: All research, weights, and training methodologies published freely</li>
<li><strong>Decentralized</strong>: No single point of control or failure</li>
<li><strong>Aligned</strong>: Built with explicit mechanisms for community governance</li>
<li><strong>Efficient</strong>: Optimized for accessibility, not just raw capability</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-technical-foundation">The Technical Foundation</h2>
<p>We're starting with a focus on three areas:</p>
<h3 id="1-distributed-training-infrastructure">1. Distributed Training Infrastructure</h3>
<p>Training large models requires significant compute. Rather than centralizing this in massive data centers, we're developing protocols for distributed training across heterogeneous hardware. Our early experiments show promising results with gradient compression and asynchronous updates.</p>
<h3 id="2-open-data-pipelines">2. Open Data Pipelines</h3>
<p>Quality training data is as important as model architecture. We're building transparent data curation pipelines with clear provenance tracking. Every piece of training data will have documented lineage.</p>
<h3 id="3-governance-mechanisms">3. Governance Mechanisms</h3>
<p>AI systems make decisions that affect people. Those people should have a voice in how these systems evolve. We're exploring on-chain governance mechanisms that give stakeholders meaningful input.</p>
<h2 id="whats-next">What's Next</h2>
<p>Over the coming months, we'll publish:</p>
<ul>
<li>Our initial architecture proposals</li>
<li>Distributed training benchmarks</li>
<li>Data curation guidelines</li>
<li>Governance framework drafts</li>
</ul>
<p>This is day one. The hard work is ahead. But we believe the destination is worth the journey.</p>
<p>Join us.</p>
